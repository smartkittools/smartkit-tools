How to reduce image size in KB

  1. Choose your photo or signature image.
  2. Tap 50, 100 or 200 KB, or type the size you need.
  3. Select Compress image, then download the result.

Common questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is compressed inside your browser and stays on your device.

চাকরির আবেদনের ছবি ১০০ KB-এর নিচে কীভাবে আনব?

ছবি বাছাই করে 100 KB বোতামে চাপুন, তারপর Compress image চাপুন। টুলটি মান ঠিক রেখে যতটা সম্ভব ছোট করে দেবে।

What if the size cannot be reached?

Very small targets may need a smaller picture. The tool lowers quality first, then shrinks the dimensions step by step until it fits.

What happens to transparent PNG images?

The result is a JPG, so transparent areas become white.
