SmartKit Tool is a growing set of simple utilities for everyday work. Writers, students and office workers use them to check length limits, tidy copied text and fix capitalization. More tools are added regularly.

Common questions

Is my text saved or sent anywhere?

No. Every tool runs inside your browser, so your text never leaves your device.

Do the tools work with Bangla text?

Yes. Word, character and line counts work with Bangla and other languages. Case conversion applies only to scripts that have upper and lower case, such as English.

How are words counted?

Any group of characters separated by spaces or line breaks counts as one word. Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute.

Is it free?

Yes, every tool is free to use.
